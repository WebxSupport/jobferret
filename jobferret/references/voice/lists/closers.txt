# closers.txt
# What this is: phrases that must not appear in the final paragraph. A closing summary,
# a stock thank-you or a stock invitation reads as a template (04 §4 "Closers to avoid").
# A hit is voice_lint check 8 (ends).
# Match semantics: one entry per line; lowercase; matched case-insensitively with word
# boundaries, but only inside the LAST paragraph of the body (the text after the final blank
# line, before the sign-off block "Yours sincerely," / "Thanks," / "Best," and the name).
# Normalise curly quotes. "overall" is checked in the last paragraph only, because it is
# ordinary English elsewhere.
#
# Not literal, so the linter handles it separately (structural):
#   Recap detection. A last paragraph that repeats three or more content words from the first
#   paragraph, or that starts "In summary" / "To sum up", is a recap. The model rewrites it to end
#   on an ask, an offer, a plain fact or a sign-off (04 §7 step 8).
# Source: 04 §4 "Closers to avoid", 04 §6.1 and §6.8 (close pairs).
