# openers.txt
# What this is: sentence-start patterns that must not open a document. They open with the
# writer's emotions, the act of writing, the job title read back, or a pleasantry (04 §4
# "Openers to avoid"). A hit is voice_lint check 8 (ends).
# Match semantics: one entry per line; lowercase; matched case-insensitively as a PREFIX of the
# first sentence of the body, after the greeting line ("Dear Hannah,", "Hi Tom,") and any
# subject line are removed. Whole-word match at the end of the entry. Normalise curly quotes.
# For email and follow-up formats, also test the first sentence after the greeting, since there
# the greeting is a separate line.
# Severity: every entry fails the check, except "as a" and "as an", which only warn. "As a
# Ferncroft customer since 2023, I..." can be a good opener; "As a passionate professional..."
# cannot. The model reads the warning and decides.
#
# Not literal, so the linter handles it separately (structural):
#   "with" + digit + "years" or "with 5+ years" at the start, for example "With 8 years of
#   experience in...". Use the regex  ^with\s+\d+\+?\s+years  . The literal entries below cover
#   the spelled-out forms ("with over", "with more than") and "with my".
# Source: 04 §4 "Openers to avoid", 04 §3 (tonal: pleasantry opener), 04 §6.2.
