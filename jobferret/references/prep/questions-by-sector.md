# Likely interview questions by sector

Used by `modes/prep.md` step 4. For each sector: the one thing its interviewers are testing for, then six to ten questions to prepare. Pick three to five from the user's sector, three to five from the posting, and two or three from the generic UK list. Each section cites its source in `../research/02-sector-application-playbooks.md` Part 2. The questions are typical forms, not a leaked list; the advert and invitation override them.

## 1. Generic UK questions (every sector)

**Competency ("tell me about a time")**: answered in STAR order, most of the time on what the user did.
- Tell me about a time you had to deliver something with a tight deadline.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a colleague or manager. What did you do?
- Give an example of a mistake you made and what you changed afterwards.
- Describe a time you had to win someone round who didn't want to change.
- Tell me about a problem you solved that others had given up on.

**Strengths-based** (used by the Civil Service and many graduate schemes; answers are judged on energy and fit, so there is no STAR structure to hide behind (02 §2.9)):
- What do you enjoy doing most at work? What drains you?
- What do you find yourself doing well without having to try?
- When have you felt most proud of something you did?

**Motivation**:
- Why this role, and why us rather than a competitor? (Prepare a reason that fails the swap test: something only this employer has.)
- Why are you leaving your current job? (One honest, forward-looking sentence. No criticism of the employer.)
- Where do you want to be in two or three years?

**Notice, salary and practicalities**:
- What is your notice period, and when could you start?
- What salary are you looking for? (Use the prep salary notes: the advertised range and the user's floor only.)
- Are you interviewing elsewhere?
- Are you happy with the working pattern (hybrid days, shifts, travel)?

**Right to work**:
- Do you have the right to work in the UK? Will you need sponsorship now or in the future? (Answer exactly as `profile.eligibility` says; never improvise eligibility.)

## 2. Software engineering and IT (02 §2.1)

**Tests for:** whether you can reason through a technical problem out loud and explain the trade-offs you made, not just recall syntax.
- Walk me through a system you built. What would you change now?
- Tell me about the hardest bug you've tracked down.
- How would you design [a small version of their product]? Where would it break first under load?
- How do you decide what to test, and what not to?
- Tell me about a code review where you disagreed with the reviewer.
- Talk me through your take-home: why this structure?
- How do you keep a deploy safe when you can't test everything?

## 3. Data, AI and ML (02 §2.2)

**Tests for:** business judgment with data: which question mattered, what decision changed, and whether the result would survive in production.
- Tell me about an analysis that changed a decision. What was the decision?
- Write or describe a SQL query to [find the top customers by month, deduplicated].
- How would you check whether this A/B test result is real?
- A stakeholder says your numbers are wrong. What do you do first?
- How did you deploy and monitor the model, and how did you know when it drifted?
- Explain [a model or metric you used] to someone non-technical.
- What would you do in your first month with our data?

## 4. Product and UX/design (02 §2.3)

**Tests for:** the reasoning behind decisions, with evidence of outcomes; the portfolio presentation is the interview.
- Walk us through one case study from problem to outcome. What did you decide, and why?
- What did the research tell you that you didn't expect?
- Tell me about a design you shipped that didn't work. What happened next?
- How do you decide between two features when you can only build one?
- What would you change about our product, and how would you measure it?
- How do you work with engineers when a design can't be built as drawn?

## 5. Marketing, comms and creative (02 §2.4)

**Tests for:** a point of view on the brand backed by results, and clean writing under time pressure.
- What would you do with our brand in the next six months?
- Tell me about a campaign: the target, the channel, the result in numbers.
- Which campaign of ours, or a competitor's, would you have done differently?
- How do you decide where to spend a limited budget?
- Talk us through your written test or channel audit.
- How do you handle a client or stakeholder who wants something you think won't work?
- How do you measure success for work that doesn't convert directly (PR, brand, internal comms)?

## 6. Sales and business development (02 §2.5)

**Tests for:** numbers you can defend (quota, attainment, deal size) and how you sell in the room, often through a mock call.
- What was your quota last year and what did you hit? How?
- Walk me through your biggest deal from first contact to close.
- Sell me [this product] (mock call or demo).
- Tell me about a deal you lost. What would you do differently?
- How do you build a pipeline from nothing in a new territory?
- How do you handle "it's too expensive"?
- What questions would you ask us about OTE, ramp and team attainment?

## 7. Finance, banking and accounting (02 §2.6)

**Tests for:** technical accuracy and qualification status stated exactly, with commercial understanding of the numbers.
- Where are you in your qualification (ACA, ACCA, CIMA, CFA), and what's left?
- Talk me through your month-end close. Where do the delays come from?
- Walk me through how the three financial statements link.
- Tell me about a variance you investigated. What caused it?
- How have you improved a finance process or report?
- (Banking) Talk me through a recent deal or market move and what it means for this division.
- How do you explain a financial result to a non-finance manager?

## 8. Legal (02 §2.7)

**Tests for:** commercial awareness and real reasons for this firm and practice area.
- Why this firm, and why this practice area?
- Tell me about a commercial story you've followed and how it affects our clients.
- Talk us through the case study: the issues, the risks, your advice.
- Tell me about a time you had to be precise under pressure.
- Where are you with the SQE (or LPC), and what's your plan?
- (Paralegal or lateral) Tell me about a matter you handled and your part in it.

## 9. Healthcare and NHS (02 §2.8)

**Tests for:** the NHS values (compassion, respect, quality of care) shown through specific patient-facing examples, and safe practice in scenarios.
- Tell me about a time you put a patient's needs first when it was difficult.
- What would you do if you saw a colleague doing something unsafe?
- A patient's condition is deteriorating and you're short-staffed. What do you do?
- Tell me about a safeguarding concern you raised, or how you would raise one.
- How do you handle a complaint from a patient or relative?
- Give an example of how you've improved care or a process on your ward or team.
- Which of the trust's values matters most to you, with an example?
- (Band 6 and above) Tell me about leading a shift or supporting a junior colleague.

## 10. Public sector and Civil Service (02 §2.9)

**Tests for:** the Success Profiles behaviours named in the advert, at the grade asked, with what you personally did.
- Tell me about a time you delivered at pace. (Delivering at Pace)
- Give an example of making an effective decision with incomplete information. (Making Effective Decisions)
- Tell me about a time you worked with others to achieve a shared goal. (Working Together)
- Describe a time you communicated something complex to a mixed audience. (Communicating and Influencing)
- Tell me about a time you changed and improved a process. (Changing and Improving)
- Tell me about a time you led a team or a piece of work through difficulty. (Leadership)
- Strengths questions: "Do you prefer starting things or finishing them?"
- (Some roles) A short presentation on a set topic, then questions.

## 11. Education and academia (02 §2.10)

**Tests for:** safeguarding and pupil progress (schools); research fit and a fundable plan (academia).
- (Schools) Tell us about a lesson that went well and one that didn't. What did you change?
- How do you make sure every pupil progresses, including those with SEND?
- What would you do if a pupil told you something that worried you? (safeguarding)
- How do you manage poor behaviour without losing the class?
- Why this school, given its ethos?
- (Academia) Summarise your research in two minutes for a non-specialist.
- What will you publish in the next three years, and where will the funding come from?
- How would you teach [a core module in the department]?

## 12. Engineering and manufacturing (02 §2.11)

**Tests for:** technical depth on real projects with scale and safety, plus registration and clearance status.
- Walk us through a project: the scale, your part, the technical problem you solved.
- Tell me about a design or process change that improved safety, quality or cost.
- How do you approach root-cause analysis on a recurring fault?
- What standards or regulations did you work to, and how did you show compliance?
- Where are you with chartership (CEng, IEng, EngTech)?
- Are you eligible for the clearance this role needs?
- Tell me about a disagreement with a contractor or client and how it was settled.

## 13. Skilled trades and logistics (02 §2.12)

**Tests for:** valid tickets and licences, safe working, and turning up when you said you would.
- Which cards and tickets do you hold, and when do they expire (CSCS, Gas Safe, 18th Edition, HGV, CPC, FLT)?
- Walk me through how you'd do [a typical job] safely.
- What would you do if you found the work wasn't to spec, or unsafe, on arrival?
- When can you start, and what hours or shifts can you do?
- Tell me about a time you had to fix a problem on site with no one to ask.
- Do you have your own tools and transport?

## 14. Hospitality, retail and customer service (02 §2.13)

**Tests for:** how you treat customers when it's busy or going wrong, and your availability.
- Tell me about a time you dealt with an angry customer.
- What would you do if the queue was long and a colleague called in sick?
- What does good service look like to you here?
- What hours and days can you work, including weekends and the run-up to Christmas?
- Tell me about a time you noticed a problem and fixed it without being asked.
- (Food) What do you know about food safety and allergens?
- (Situational judgment test) Rank these responses to a customer situation.

## 15. Executive and senior leadership (02 §2.14)

**Tests for:** scope and results you can prove (P&L, headcount, turnarounds) and judgment the board would trust.
- What was the size of your remit (budget, P&L, people, geographies), and what changed under you?
- Tell me about a strategic decision that didn't work. What did you learn?
- How would you approach your first 100 days here?
- Tell me about a time you had to take a team through serious change or cuts.
- How do you work with a board or a CEO you disagree with?
- What would your last three direct reports say about you?
- What are your expectations on package, notice and restrictive covenants?

## 16. Graduates and early career (02 §2.15)

**Tests for:** a different, real example for each competency, from study, work or societies, and a clear reason for this employer.
- Why this scheme, and why this firm over its competitors?
- Tell me about a time you worked in a team that wasn't working well.
- Give an example of when you took the lead without being asked.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.
- What is a current issue facing our industry?
- (Assessment centre) Group exercise, in-tray exercise or presentation: prepare how you'll contribute without dominating.
- (Recorded video) The same competency questions, timed, with no second take.

## 17. Career changers (02 §2.16)

**Tests for:** proof of commitment to the new field (projects, training) and skills that transfer with evidence.
- Why are you changing career, and why now?
- What have you done to learn this field? Show me something you've made.
- Which of your past experience applies here? Give an example.
- How will you handle being less senior than you were?
- What will you find hardest, and how will you deal with it?
- Why should we take you over someone with direct experience?

## 18. Contractors and freelancers (02 §2.17)

**Tests for:** that you can do the job from day one, with clear availability, rate and IR35 position.
- Walk me through your last two or three contracts: the client, the problem, what you delivered.
- When are you available, and what's your day rate?
- What's your position on inside or outside IR35 for this role?
- How do you get up to speed in a new client's codebase or systems in week one?
- Tell me about a contract that went wrong. How did you handle the client?
- Have you been put forward for this client by another agency? (Answer truthfully; double submission can rule you out.)

## 19. Remote-first roles (02 §2.18)

**Tests for:** working on your own and in writing: async habits, self-management, and time-zone fit.
- How do you structure your day when no one is checking in?
- Tell me about a time a written message caused a misunderstanding. What did you change?
- How do you keep a distributed team informed about your work?
- Which hours can you overlap with [the team's time zone]?
- Where will you work from, and do you have the right to work there?
- (Written or async interview) Answer in a document by a deadline: treat it as a writing sample.

## 20. Management consulting and strategy (02 §2.19)

**Tests for:** structured problem-solving with accurate numbers in the case, and real reasons for this firm and office.
- Case: our client's profits fell 20% last year. How would you find out why?
- Estimate [a market size] out loud.
- Read this chart: what's the one thing the client should do?
- Why consulting, why this firm, and why this office?
- Tell me about a time you led a group to a decision.
- Tell me about a time you persuaded someone senior to change their mind.

## 21. Charity and third sector (02 §2.20)

**Tests for:** commitment to the mission backed by evidence, and the person specification worked through criterion by criterion.
- Why this charity, and what do you know about our strategy?
- How do your values connect to our work? Give an example, not a statement.
- Tell me about something you achieved with very limited resources.
- How would you measure the impact of [a programme in the role]?
- Tell me about working with volunteers or trustees.
- (Fundraising) What's the largest gift or grant you've secured, and how?
- (Task or presentation) Often set for the second interview: check the brief.

## 22. Pharma, biotech and regulated industries (02 §2.21)

**Tests for:** specific regulatory knowledge (the regulator, the document type, the procedure), not general "GxP experience".
- Which regulators and guidelines have you worked under (MHRA, FDA, EMA; GMP, GCP, ICH-GCP E6)?
- Talk me through a deviation and the CAPA you raised.
- Tell me about an audit or inspection you prepared for. What did they find?
- Walk me through a submission or protocol you contributed to.
- How do you make sure an SOP is followed when the team is under pressure?
- (Scientists) Present your research: the question, the method, the result, what's next.
- What can you tell us about your previous projects without breaching confidentiality?
